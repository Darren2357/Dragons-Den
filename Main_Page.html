Xiao Long Bao

Xiao Long Bao are delicate steamed soup dumplings filled with seasoned pork and rich, savoury broth, all wrapped in a thin, tender dough skin. Each dumpling is carefully pleated and steamed in a traditional bamboo basket, creating a delicate balance of texture and flavour.

A Taste of Shanghai's History are believed to have originated during the late Qing Dynasty in the 19th century. Originally known as Nanxiang soup buns, they gradually became one of Shanghai's most recognisable culinary specialties.
